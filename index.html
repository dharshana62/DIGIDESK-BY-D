The Immigrant Architect's US Survival Guide

Curated by Dharshana // Solutions Architecture

1. Drawing Sets & Project Phases

US projects run through standard milestones: SD (Schematic Design), DD (Design Development), CD (Construction Documents), and CA (Construction Administration). Sheet naming follows CSI/AIA conventions: A-100 (Plans), A-200 (Elevations), A-300 (Sections), A-500 (Details).

2. Imperial Units & Nominal vs Actual Lumber

If transitioning from metric: 1 inch = 25.4mm, 1 foot = 12 inches. A "2x4" stud is actually 1-1/2" × 3-1/2", and a "2x6" is 1-1/2" × 5-1/2". Always annotate dimensions from face of stud or grid line, not drywall finish.

3. NCARB AXP & ARE 5.0 Licensure

Start logging your Architectural Experience Program (AXP) hours from day one. You need 3,740 total hours across Practice Management, Project Management, Programming & Analysis, Project Planning & Design, Project Development & Documentation, and Construction & Evaluation.

4. Visa Strategies for Foreign Architecture Grads

Verify whether your architecture degree is STEM-designated (3-year OPT). Initiate H-1B lottery sponsorship conversations with firm leadership early in Q4 before the March filing window. Keep a detailed design portfolio for potential O-1 Extraordinary Ability pathways.
